01 — About

AI scientists for disease

AI in disease biology has largely been the domain of classification models. With agentic AI and virtual modeling, we can now ask a different question: how do we build AI scientists that construct, simulate, and revise mechanistic models of disease?

The next generation of AI scientists should infer generative models of complex systems — virtual cells, virtual brains, virtual organisms — whose behaviour can be simulated, perturbed, and interrogated. These models encode explicit hypotheses about how components interact and evolve, forming a closed loop: model → prediction → experiment → model revision. DREAM brings together the methods needed to close that loop and the disease-biology domains where they must operate.

Generative modeling — neural ODEs/SDEs, flow matching
Geometric & topological deep learning
AI agents & foundation models for scientific reasoning
Lab-in-the-loop experimental design
